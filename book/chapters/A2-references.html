References

The results this book states are, with few exceptions, results that belong to the literature rather than to the toolkit, and where that is so the source is cited at the point of use rather than reproduced. The list below collects those sources. It is deliberately short: it names the paper or the book where a reader who wants the argument in full will find it, and prefers a standard textbook treatment to the original announcement wherever the textbook is the more useful place to be sent.
