statmodels7

Distributional regression with exact derivatives

Every family in the toolkit carries its derivatives to the fifth order in closed form. Any parameter can have smooth, random, penalized, break-point, score-driven or regime-switching terms, and every fit reports whether it converged.

Example

The number of calls of barn owl nestlings in 27 nests (Owls, from glmmTMB). The counts are negative binomial. The mean has a smooth effect of the arrival time of the parent and a random effect of the nest, and the dispersion theta has an equation of its own.

data(Owls, package = "glmmTMB")
fit <- statmod(SiblingNegotiation ~ FoodTreatment * SexParent +
                 offset(logBroodSize) +
                 s(ArrivalTime, smoother = bspline_smooth(k = 10)) +
                 random(~ 1 | Nest) |
                 theta ~ FoodTreatment + random(~ 1 | Nest),
               distrib = negbin2_distrib(), data = Owls)
statmod_certificate(fit)$state
#> [1] "converged"

summary(fit)

The book

statmodels7: Statistical models built from reusable parts covers linear, generalized linear, additive, mixed, penalized, break-point, nonlinear and time-series models fitted with statmod(), and how to write a new distribution, link, model term and optimizer.

Packages

  • statmodels7: statmod(), the criteria for the hyperparameters, inference and simulation
  • modelterms7: model terms and the formula interpreter
  • distributions7: distributions with derivatives to the fifth order
  • linkfunctions7: link functions
  • optimizers7: optimizers and stopping rules
  • penalties7: penalties
  • basis7: bases and smoothers
  • parameters7: constrained parameters
  • numericals7: numerical routines
